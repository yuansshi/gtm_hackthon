Employment graph reasoning now supports source-reviewed published people and exact-version owner connection attestations. Native Kuzu current/former-employer traversals use reviewed domain identities and display supporting passages. Stale, changed, withdrawn or superseded evidence and revoked attestations cannot support warm paths. This is inspectable possible-introduction reasoning, not independently established employment overlap, acquaintance, authority or willingness. Shared-education paths now have a separate reviewed source/identity workflow and native traversal; independently confirmed education, overlap, acquaintance, buying-cycle reasoning and broad live telemetry remain incomplete. See [People and introductions](people-introductions.md).
