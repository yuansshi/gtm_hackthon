# Adjacent segment review and search handoff

**Product & ICP → Ideal customers → Adjacent segments** supports Accept, Adjust and Dismiss for the selected business’s catalog-linked suggestions. Adjust opens an inline name/rationale editor. Review the original catalog link and acknowledge that the revised scope is a research hypothesis before applying it. Cancel leaves the profile untouched. Restoring the catalog suggestion clears its acceptance and requires a fresh choice.

Original IDs, source links and catalog reasoning remain available; an adjustment cannot replace the citation or introduce another business’s suggestion. The adjusted name is limited to 120 characters and the internal rationale to 600. Neither accepting nor adjusting verifies a new market, technical compatibility or customer demand. This control does not independently fetch or validate the source. Optional model ICP proposals remain a separate reviewed path; autonomous evidence-backed adjacent-market discovery is still incomplete.

Decisions and optional adjustments persist with the business profile. Applying, dismissing or restoring scope revokes research and unsent-message approvals. Merely opening or canceling the editor does not write a profile. Legacy profiles without adjustment keys retain their exact approval hashes and original accepted-segment export shape.

After profile approval, **Accounts → Search prospects → Adjacent segment** can select one accepted segment. The default is Current ICP only. The selected name enters the displayed query text, which must be acknowledged before a project Tavily request. The server recomputes the exact plan, checks the accepted business-scoped segment and binds request replay to it. A changed/dismissed segment or changed query cannot silently reuse a prior plan. Completed search history keeps its original target.

Search remains limited to the current US equipment OEM scope, one or two approved product families, at most two provider queries, three company domains and two pages per domain. Domain exclusions remain local. Selecting a service-oriented suggestion does not remove the OEM requirement. Source retrieval still creates proposals only: company identity, geography, manufacturer status and application require exact-quote review before account admission; admission never automatically shortlists or verifies buying intent.

Internal adjustment rationale stays out of prospect queries, Band packets, native graph context/facts and outbound draft-model context. Owner exports and explicitly acknowledged intake-model context retain the research interpretation. Outbound draft context may retain the chosen segment name as an unverified research target, not a supplier capability or validated fit. Source citations still require human interpretation.

Twelve dedicated tests cover legacy hashes/export shape, business/schema bounds, fixed citations, approval and isolation, reasoning privacy, bounded query scope, exact-plan/request replay, changed/dismissed/restored plans, source-review admission, in-flight invalidation/refunds, strict HTTP, intercepted real-SDK/Band payloads and store reopen persistence. The isolated deployment probe retains an adjusted decision across process restart. Main Tavily and other live integrations remain disabled; fixture success does not establish live adjacent-market discovery.
